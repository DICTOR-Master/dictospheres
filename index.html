What's new

  • 16 Oct 2026 The DICTO Ogdoecontahedron published (#31, DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23404688): the Spheres of Dharma turned inside out give a new solid of 20 regular hexagons and 60 kites, the dual of the Hecatodihedron; its hexagon-joined packings form pyrochlore networks.
  • 15 Oct 2026 A Fibonacci Spine published (#30, DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23396361): five periodic packings of the DICTO Ogdoecontahedron meet round a five-fold axis, and the copies they must give up along the axis form, exactly, the canonical Fibonacci chain, its window set by a five-fold ring clashing with itself. Explore it in Kaleidohedra’s new Golden Bodhi Pyrochlore Networks world: the pyrochlore networks, the five voices and the chorus.
  • 15 Oct 2026 Theaetetus published (#29, DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23378070): an icosahedron held exactly by its Cleo-Icos, grown to the DICTO Hecatodihedron; a new world in Kaleidohedra.
  • 15 Oct 2026 Five papers get their own DOIs: the Euclid–Kepler–Pacioli Cell (#8), the DICTO Dodeca-13 (#13), the DICTO-Star (#14), Kagome Hulls (#17) and the DICTO Hexa (#18).
  • 14 Oct 2026 The DICTO Truncated-Icosahedral Hecatodihedron published (#28, DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23360858): a convex solid of 102 faces from the Spheres of Dharma.
  • 14 Oct 2026 The FUJI Family published (#20, DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23357896): pyramids on flat-ended triangles, the antiprism rule, and Cleo-Icos & Antonius, which fill space two to one.
  • 14 Oct 2026 AXE, CLEO and FUJI published (#19, 10.5281/zenodo.23351291): the three gap pieces of the Lotus seed; AXE and CLEO are Mosseri–Sadoc tiles, FUJI is new, with printable nets.
  • 14 Oct 2026 Capturing the In-betweens published (#27, 10.5281/zenodo.23345502): the in-between stages of DICTO's nested structures pass through the great stellated dodecahedron, the excavated dodecahedron and the rhombic hexecontahedron, and a new convex solid, the DICTO Truncated-Icosahedral Hecatodihedron (102 faces, all tangent to one sphere). With cut-out nets, a new Polyhedraverse group and a build movie in the app.
  • 14 Oct 2026 DICTO now has its own DOI, DICTO: Collected Geometric Works, gathering all the papers: 10.5281/zenodo.23345699.
  • 13 Oct 2026 DICTO ATOM-13×13 published (#26, 10.5281/zenodo.23333183): thirteen clusters of twelve around one, with 24 buildable kits.
  • 13 Oct 2026 DICTO Spheroid-13 RD published (#25, 10.5281/zenodo.23332821): a nearly round, keyed block of thirteen rhombic dodecahedra that fills space by translation.
  • 12 Oct 2026 The Chain of Karma published (#22), with its data: 10.5281/zenodo.23310541.
  • 12 Oct 2026 The Bodhi Seed published (#24, 10.5281/zenodo.23311353): a rhombic triacontahedron holds 12 smaller ones and one at its centre, the Lotus seed's pattern; its gap fills exactly with three convex piece types, in two ways (checked).
  • 12 Oct 2026 Spheres of Dharma published (#23, 10.5281/zenodo.23310833): a second endless golden cycle, icosahedron, icosidodecahedron, dodecahedron, fullerene cage, rhombic triacontahedron, icosahedron, every layer filled exactly (237 pieces per cycle, checked).
  • 12 Oct 2026 The icosidodecahedron as the meeting place of the icosahedron and dodecahedron: in their dual compound, their 30 edge pairs cross at its corners (checked). The fullerene cage wraps the dodecahedron at exactly one golden step.
  • 11 Oct 2026 The Chain of Karma (#22): the icosahedron and dodecahedron as endless shells of each other, checked to 20,497 pieces; the Lotus seed's surface carries the C60 fullerene pattern; its 13 icosahedra lie on the 600-cell's first shell.
  • 11 Oct 2026 The three apps now share one geometry library (krp-core), every shape and lattice in one place.
  • 11 Oct 2026 DICTO Icosa, a new world in Kaleidohedra: the Lotus seed, UNITY, the Stella-Corona and the Kepler Star Diadem.
