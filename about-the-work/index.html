About the work

DICTO's online work has three faces.

The books

The books set out the ideas: how space is filled, how order changes from one shell or layer to the next, and why some shapes can fill space while others cannot. They form one series, The Five Orders of Space.

The proofs

The proofs make those ideas certain. Every claim is checked exactly, and the central results are proved in Lean, a language in which a computer verifies each step down to the axioms. Several are registered on the Palomar registry of machine-checked mathematics.

The apps

The apps are where the ideas become tangible. In Kaleidohedra, Rhombiverse and Polyhedraverse you can hold the shapes, turn them, and pull them apart to see how they are built, piece by piece, as a sculptor would. Each one carries the theorems of the books and the checked calculations behind them.
